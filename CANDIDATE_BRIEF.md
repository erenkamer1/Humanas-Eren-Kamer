# Junior Software Developer — Take-Home Exercise

Thanks for applying to Humanas.

## About Humanas

Humanas helps people discover their potential and connect with opportunities where they can thrive. We also help organizations make better, fairer talent decisions by combining behavioral science, gamified assessments, our MetaCV technology, and an AI-powered BI Platform. Our engineering team primarily builds with C#/.NET.

## The Exercise

The exercise is available in the repository below:

`starter-repo/README.md`

The core task is intentionally small and should take around one hour to complete. Beyond that, it's entirely up to you whether you'd like to extend your solution with additional improvements. There is no expectation that you do so, and no stopwatch either way.

You are free to use any programming language, framework, or tooling you prefer. AI-assisted development tools are also allowed. However, you should be prepared to explain your implementation decisions and approach during the follow-up discussion.

## Timeline

Please submit your solution within **3 business days** of receiving this exercise. This helps keep the exercise fresh in your mind when we discuss it afterward—it is not intended as a time pressure.

Start whenever you can dedicate focused time to it. If you expect to start later because of work, school, or other commitments, or if you need additional time, simply let us know. Clear communication is appreciated and reflects the kind of judgment we value.

## Submission

1. Clone this repository to your local machine.
2. Create a **private GitHub repository** under your own GitHub account.
3. Commit and push your solution to your private repository.
4. Add the Humanas GitHub users provided in the accompanying email as **collaborators**.
5. Complete the submission document requested in this repository and include it in your repository.
6. Reply through the same communication channel you received this exercise from and share the URL of your private repository.

We look forward to reviewing your solution.
